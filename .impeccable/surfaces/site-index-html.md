## Scope

The public website at ahamedzoha.github.io/mezzanine (one page, plus a changelog and a 404). Mode:
Persuade. The owner picked "One evening" over "Lights down" and "Press a key" (pinned in plan mode;
no roll). Code-led. A whole surface inside the settled Bias Light world.

Audience/job: someone with a Plex server and an Apple silicon Mac deciding whether to download it,
then getting past Gatekeeper. Proof: real pictures of the app playing open films; real keys; the
original file played on the Mac. Constraints: no studio artwork, no Plex logo, no invented quotes or
numbers, no third-party requests, works without JavaScript. Donations only after Sponsors approval.

## Direction contract

THESIS: the page is one evening, told by the clock. Features appear at the minute you'd use them.
It refuses the headline, split hero and feature-card grid of every player's site.

OWN-WORLD: Bias Light. Graphite room, warm ink, one lilac (Download). All other colour comes from the
film in the picture. Big Shoulders Display 800 for the headline and the clock, system face for the
rest. Left-aligned on the gutter, hairline plus a ruled caption of facts. No cards, no glow edges.

STORY: it's 21:02 and you want to watch something. By 22:40 you know how the app behaves at every
step of a night, that it plays the original file, and that it's free. You download it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: graphite room lit by Sintel's colours from behind the app window. Headline top left
on the gutter in title-card type, facts caption under a hairline, lilac Download and a quiet GitHub
link in one row. The Home window starts below, wider than the text column, and runs past the fold and
the right edge. The clock, 21:02, stands in the left margin and stays with you.

SIGNATURE: the clock in the margin runs as you scroll, minute by minute, through real running times;
the room dims as it gets later. At 21:12 the W key is a real button: press it and the picture goes
back and shows its subtitle.

FORM: a running order (evening programme), code-led. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished.
