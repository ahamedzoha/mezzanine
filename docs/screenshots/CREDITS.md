# Film credits

The screenshots show Mezzanine playing open films by Blender and friends, cropped and framed. Posters are
made from the films' own pictures with the title set in Big Shoulders Display (SIL OFL). Progress, names
and the library are invented. Pictures that show *Caminandes: Gran Dillama* are available under CC BY-SA 3.0.
